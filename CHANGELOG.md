# Changelog

The app's releases. Each one's APK, SHA-256 and signing certificate are on the [Releases](https://github.com/wen-da-ng/otterbridge-allsense/releases) page.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-20)

The first release.

- **Explorer:** motion and environment sensors, every camera and lens, microphones, GNSS with a sky plot, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, battery and hardware features.
- **Streaming** over Wi-Fi, a USB cable or Bluetooth LE: the browser dashboard, the Python, C++ and Rust clients, the ROS 2 bridge, Arduino/ESP32 boards, MQTT.
- **Cameras and microphones** as MJPEG or H.264 video and live audio.
- **For robots:** mount transform (robot axes and attitude), AprilTag detection with a live view, recordings on the phone, commands (screen, speaker, vibration, torch).
- Speaks AllSense protocol version 0.
